# Arthur Voice Profile

## Purpose

This note defines the target speaking style for Arthur so future HTS and neural work can be judged against the same character brief.

Arthur does not need a premium assistant voice.
He needs a pleasant, understandable, socially warm robot voice that feels intentional.

## Core Character

- male
- youthful
- playful
- calm
- partly robotic
- partly natural

## Product Goal

Arthur is a robot who wants to interact comfortably with humans.

That means the voice should feel:

- approachable rather than authoritative
- friendly rather than overly excited
- articulate rather than theatrical
- synthetic by design, but not cold or harsh

## Desired Listening Traits

- clear intelligibility
- steady pacing
- light, bright tone
- crisp consonants
- moderate warmth
- short, clean pauses
- restrained emotional swing

## Traits To Avoid

- deep or booming delivery
- overly polished audiobook realism
- sleepy or slow pacing
- stern assistant energy
- childish cartoon exaggeration
- metallic harshness
- obviously uncanny human imitation

## HTS Implications

If Arthur is implemented with `HTS`, tuning should bias toward:

- slightly faster speaking rate than Leena
- brighter tone and lighter perceived age
- clean articulation
- mild synthetic sheen instead of heavy realism
- prosody that stays stable across ordinary robot dialogue

## Neural Implications

If Arthur is implemented with a neural voice, source selection should prefer:

- a young or youthful-sounding male speaker
- clean recording conditions
- steady phrasing
- friendly tone without dramatic affect
- medium pitch rather than very low pitch
- speech that already feels approachable and lightly curious

## Success Standard

Arthur succeeds when listeners feel:

- the robot is easy to understand
- the robot sounds pleasant to spend time with
- the robot clearly has personality
- the robot does not need to pass as fully human
